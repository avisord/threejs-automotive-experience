Credits

The showroom is built on other people's generous work. Here's everything it uses, who made it, and the licence it's shared under.

Car models

All from Sketchfab. We changed them: compressed the files (meshopt geometry, WebP textures), set their orientation, scale and glass, and let you repaint, re-material and move their parts.

    Models under CC BY-NC-SA are used non-commercially, and our adapted versions of them are shared under the same licence. Car makes, models and designs are trademarks of their owners; this is a non-commercial fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by any of them.

    Models you upload yourself stay in your browser and are never sent anywhere.
